# User Guide

## Getting Started

After installing the extension, click the InterviewPrep icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the popup. On first launch, choose a subject to begin.

## Modes

### Study Mode
Browse questions topic by topic at your own pace.

1. Select a subject from the Home or Subjects screen.
2. Tap a topic to enter Study mode.
3. Read the question, select an answer (or write one for open-ended questions), then submit.
4. Review the explanation and key concepts before moving to the next question.

For open-ended (text) questions, read the explanation and use the **I knew this** / **Didn't know** buttons to self-assess — this counts toward your progress score.

### Interview Simulation
A timed quiz that scores you like a real interview.

1. From a subject's Topics screen, tap **Practice Interview**.
2. Configure the number of questions, difficulty filter, and time limit.
3. Answer questions under time pressure — unanswered questions count as incorrect.
4. Review your results and per-question breakdown when the session ends.

## Progress
The **Progress** tab shows your overall correct rate and per-topic completion across all study sessions and interviews.

To reset all progress, scroll to the bottom of the Progress screen and tap **Reset all progress**.

## Bookmarks
Tap the bookmark icon on any question during Study mode to save it. Access saved questions from the **Bookmarks** tab.

## Settings (Top Bar)
- **A / A** — decrease or increase font size (8 levels)
- **Sun / Moon** — toggle dark and light theme
- **Expand icon** — widen the popup from 480px to 800px

## Uploading Custom Subjects
On the Subjects screen, tap **Upload Subject JSON** to import a custom question pack. The JSON must follow the InterviewPrep subject pack format. Uploaded subjects can be removed at any time by tapping the delete button next to the subject name.

## Data & Privacy
All your data (progress, bookmarks, preferences) is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
